RECENT ARTICLES I WROTE

Entropy, Cross-Entropy, and KL-Divergence


Observing a variable gives us information about the probability distribution it is drawn from, and entropy, cross-entropy, and KL divergence provide a principled way to quantify uncertainty and the cost of modeling that distribution incorrectly.

One Entropy, Two Languages: Entropy in Information Theory and Thermodynamics


The same notion of entropy underlies both information theory and thermodynamics, despite their different interpretations. In both cases, it quantifies uncertainty about the true state of a system.
